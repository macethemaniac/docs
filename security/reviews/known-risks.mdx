title: "Known risks"
description: "Risk categories Lotus tracks publicly."
---

Lotus depends on multiple venues, market data sources, wallets, and execution paths.

This page explains the main risk categories that can affect users and builders.

## Venue dependency

Lotus can be affected by venue outages, degraded APIs, delayed fills, changed rules, or paused markets.

Mitigation: venue capability checks, readiness checks, route fallbacks, and fail-closed execution.

## Rule drift

Two markets that looked compatible can diverge if a venue updates rules, resolution source, timing, or outcome definitions.

Mitigation: venue-sourced metadata, rule compatibility checks, shared outcome core validation, and conservative blocking when metadata is low quality.

## Price freshness

Display prices and execution quotes have different safety requirements.

Mitigation: display can use trusted live snapshots, but execution requires fresh quote and readiness validation.

## Execution drift

A venue orderbook can move between preview, signature, and submit.

Mitigation: slippage tolerance, order policy, quote expiry, EST binding, and fail-closed submit guards.

## Settlement mismatch

A venue can report status late or differently from expected.

Mitigation: settlement verification, per-leg receipts, idempotent reconciliation, and recovery review.

## Funding readiness mismatch

Visible balances can differ from venue-ready balances.

Mitigation: funding readiness checks, activation state tracking, in-flight reservations, and withdrawal safety rules.

## Information safety

Documentation and receipts can leak sensitive details if they include credentials, raw signed payloads, or unnecessary provider data.

Mitigation: redact sensitive values, use placeholders in examples, and keep receipts focused on the execution facts users need.

<Note>
  Known risks help readers understand the trust model and the controls Lotus uses to reduce risk.
</Note>
