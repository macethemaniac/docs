---
title: "Revenue distribution"
description: "How realized surplus and improvement fees move to users, builders, and Lotus."
---

Revenue distribution happens after verified fills.

Lotus does not distribute protocol or builder revenue from an estimated preview. It uses verified fills and the active fee policy.

## Distribution order

1. Verify venue fills.
2. Recalculate realized price improvement from verified fills.
3. Apply share improvement fee (charged at execution time, confirmed post-fill).
4. Apply price improvement fee policy and caps.
5. Allocate builder fee, if configured.
6. Leave remaining improvement with the user.
7. Write ledger entries.

## Fee types in the distribution

| Fee | Source | When settled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share improvement fee | Extra shares beyond single-venue capacity | At execution |
| Price improvement fee | Better price versus single-venue baseline | Post settlement verification |
| Execution fee | Flat bps on filled notional | At execution |
| Builder fee | Configured share of price improvement | Post settlement verification |

## Distribution formula

```text
S_net_user = S_price - priceImprovementFee - builderFee
```

The user retains all filled shares, including the extra shares unlocked by multi-venue routing. The share improvement fee is charged on the USDC value of those extra shares — not on the shares themselves.

`F_lp` is optional and applies only if a liquidity partner rebate policy is active.

## Who receives what

| Recipient | Receives |
| --- | --- |
| User | All filled shares, plus retained price improvement. |
| Builder | Configured builder fee on price improvement, if eligible. |
| Lotus protocol | Price improvement fee, share improvement fee, and execution fee per active policy. |
| LP | Configured rebate, if an RFQ policy applies. |

## When revenue is zero

Revenue should be zero when:

- The order fails.
- The route does not improve the baseline.
- Settlement verification is incomplete.
- The fee policy does not apply.
- Fees would make the user worse off on price improvement.

Share improvement fees apply independently — if extra shares were filled through multi-venue routing, the fee applies even when there is no price improvement.

## Accounting entries

Each distribution should produce ledger entries:

- `SURPLUS_REALIZED`
- `PRICE_IMPROVEMENT_FEE`
- `SHARE_IMPROVEMENT_FEE` (if applicable)
- `EXECUTION_FEE`
- `USER_SURPLUS_RETAINED`
- `PROTOCOL_FEE`
- `BUILDER_FEE` (if applicable)
- `LP_REBATE` or `LP_FEE` (if applicable)

These entries should reference the same execution ID and fee policy version.

<Warning>
  Distribution should be auditable and replay-safe. Duplicate settlement events must not duplicate revenue.
</Warning>
