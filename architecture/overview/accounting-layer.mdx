---
title: "Accounting layer"
description: "How Lotus records fills, positions, surplus, and fees."
---

The accounting layer is the system of record for executed trades.

It reconciles venue fills into user positions, balances, receipts, surplus, and fee entries.

## Responsibilities

The accounting layer records:

- Trade fills.
- Per-leg venue results.
- Settlement verification status.
- Position updates.
- Balance updates.
- Surplus calculations.
- Protocol and builder fees.
- Final execution receipts.

## Why it is separate

Execution asks venues to fill an order.

Accounting verifies what happened and records the final result.

This separation protects users when a venue fill arrives late, a route partially fills, or a status update changes after the first submit response.

## Revenue boundary

Lotus should recognize surplus and protocol revenue only after:

1. The order fills.
2. Settlement verification passes.
3. Realized surplus is positive.
4. The fee policy applies.
5. The user remains better off after fees.

## User-facing output

The accounting layer powers:

- Trade history.
- Portfolio positions.
- Funding history.
- Execution receipts.
- PnL and performance views.

<Warning>
  Accounting should be derived from verified execution and settlement data, not from frontend state.
</Warning>
