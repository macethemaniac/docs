---
title: "Position accounting"
description: "How Lotus keeps portfolio state consistent after execution."
---

Position accounting reconciles venue fills into canonical portfolio state.

It makes sure the user's trade history, positions, balances, and performance views agree with verified venue results.

## Accounting flow

1. Venue reports a fill or status update.
2. Lotus verifies the fill against the execution route.
3. Venue position records are updated.
4. The unified position is recalculated.
5. Portfolio value and performance are updated.
6. The execution receipt is finalized.

## What can go wrong

Position accounting protects against:

- Venue fills that remain pending in Lotus.
- Fills that update history but not positions.
- Positions marked from stale or unavailable data.
- Duplicate fills from repeated events.
- Sellable amount mismatches.

## Marking positions

Position marks are display values.

They help users understand current value, but they should not authorize execution. Sell execution still needs fresh route and position checks.

<Note>
  Portfolio display can use marks, but trade execution uses verified venue readiness.
</Note>
