---
title: "Route discovery"
description: "How Lotus finds candidate routes before scoring them."
---

Route discovery builds the candidate set for a user intent.

It does not choose the winning route. It enumerates the possible safe shapes that the optimization step can compare.

## Inputs

Route discovery uses:

- Canonical market and outcome.
- Side and amount.
- Venue preference.
- Available venue mappings.
- Live venue price snapshots.
- Venue depth.
- RFQ quote candidates.
- Funding and position readiness.

## Candidate shapes

Lotus can discover:

- Single-venue routes.
- Pair routes.
- Tri routes.
- RFQ-backed routes.
- Fallback candidates.

Every candidate must stay inside the shared outcome core.

## Venue preference

`BEST_ROUTE` lets Lotus discover all eligible venues.

A venue-specific preference should stay on that venue. If the requested venue is unavailable, Lotus should return unavailable instead of silently routing somewhere else.

## Discovery output

Each candidate includes:

- Route type.
- Venue legs.
- Side and amount per leg.
- Expected price.
- Available size.
- Readiness summary.
- Any safety blocker.

The next step is route optimization.
