---
title: "Route transparency"
description: "How Lotus makes routing decisions understandable."
---

Route transparency lets users and builders understand why Lotus selected a route.

The normal UI should stay simple. Advanced route details and receipts can show the evidence behind the decision.

## What route details should show

Route details can include:

- Selected route type.
- Eligible venues.
- Per-leg allocation.
- Expected average price.
- Reference route.
- Estimated savings.
- Fee impact.
- Slippage tolerance.
- Required actions.
- Readiness summary.

## What receipts should show

Receipts should show what actually happened:

- Executed venue legs.
- Filled size.
- Fill price.
- Final average price.
- Final fees.
- Realized surplus.
- Settlement verification result.

## Why transparency matters

Lotus can route across several venues and liquidity sources.

Without route transparency, a user cannot tell whether the route improved the outcome or merely added complexity. The receipt should make the final result auditable.

## Display versus execution

Display prices can be fast and smooth.

Execution routes require stricter evidence. Route transparency should preserve this distinction so users understand why a market can show a price while an order still requires fresh validation.
