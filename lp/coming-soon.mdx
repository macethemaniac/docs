Lotus LP is a liquidity product being built on top of the Lotus execution infrastructure.

It is designed for participants who want to deploy capital into prediction market liquidity, earn returns from that activity, and access products that would otherwise be unavailable in a fragmented venue landscape.

## What to expect

Lotus LP will offer capital deployment options connected to the Lotus routing and execution layer.

Participants who join early will help shape the product. Details on structure, eligibility, minimums, and returns will be shared closer to launch.

## Early exit facility

One of the products enabled by Lotus LP infrastructure is an early exit facility for prediction market positions.

Prediction markets resolve at a future date. Traders who want liquidity before resolution typically have no clean exit path — especially in markets with thin secondary orderbooks.

The early exit facility will allow traders to close a position before market resolution at a fair price, without waiting on a counterparty in the open orderbook.

<Note>
  The early exit facility is a planned feature. It is not yet live. Pricing, eligibility, and terms will be published before launch.
</Note>
