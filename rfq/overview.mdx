---
title: "Overview"
description: "How Lotus RFQs work."
---

Lotus RFQ is a structured request-for-quote flow that connects liquidity providers (LPs) to canonical market demand routed through Lotus.

When book depth is insufficient or an LP can quote inside the book, Lotus broadcasts an RFQ to eligible LPs. LPs respond with quotes; Lotus selects the dominating quote and binds it to an EST for execution.

LPs benefit from:

- **Pre-funded counterparties** — Lotus only routes against pre-funded users.
- **Scoped flow** — each RFQ is bound to a canonical market and size.
- **Structured selection** — deterministic scoring based on price, latency, and reliability.
