---
title: "What is Lotus?"
description: "Lotus is the execution and intelligence layer above prediction market venues."
---

Lotus is a prediction market execution and intelligence layer.

Instead of forcing users to compare liquidity across multiple venues, Lotus:

- **Discovers** markets across supported venues
- **Normalizes** equivalent markets into one canonical market
- **Evaluates** whether a route is safely executable
- **Finds** the best executable route
- **Executes** across supported venues

<Note>
  Lotus is **not** a prediction market venue. Lotus is the execution layer **above** prediction market venues.
</Note>

Lotus thinks like a smart order router built for resolution-bound markets: every routing decision is bound by rule compatibility, shared outcome coverage, and fail-closed safety.
