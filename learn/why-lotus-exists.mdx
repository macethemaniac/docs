Prediction market liquidity is fragmented.

The same market may exist across multiple venues with different:

- Prices
- Liquidity
- Rules
- Depth

A trader who wants exposure to a single outcome is forced to compare venues, evaluate rule equivalence, and accept whatever execution a single venue can give them.

Lotus helps users trade **one canonical market** while routing intelligently underneath.

## The Lotus thesis

1. Prediction markets will keep multiplying.
2. Liquidity will stay fragmented.
3. Users want a single market, not a venue picker.
4. Routing must be safe, transparent, and verifiable.

The execution layer is the natural home for this complexity.
