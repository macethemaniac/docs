---
title: "Canonical Markets"
description: "One market across many venues."
---

A **canonical market** is Lotus's normalized representation of an outcome that may exist on multiple venues. Users trade the canonical market; Lotus routes underneath.

Canonicalization combines:

1. [Shared Outcome Core](/learn/concepts/shared-outcome-core) — the underlying question and outcome space.
2. [Rule Compatibility](/learn/concepts/rule-compatibility) — equivalence of resolution rules.
3. **Time alignment** — overlap in resolution windows.
