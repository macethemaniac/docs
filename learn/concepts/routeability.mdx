---
title: "Routeability"
description: "Whether a route can be safely executed."
---

**Routeability** is the determination that a candidate route can be safely executed. A route is routeable when:

1. All legs share an outcome core.
2. All venues are live and quoting.
3. Rule compatibility holds for the targeted outcome.
4. Replay and scope protections are satisfiable.
5. User slippage and risk preferences can be honored.

If any check fails, the route is non-routeable.
