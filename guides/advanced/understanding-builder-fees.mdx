---
title: "Understanding builder fees"
description: "How builder fees fit into route pricing."
---

Builder fees are fees charged by an application or integration that routes users through Lotus.

They must be included in the preview so the user can understand the final expected output.

## Where builder fees apply

Builder fees can apply to:

- A successful execution.
- A route that produces price improvement.
- A configured integration or partner account.
- A specific fee policy agreed with the builder.

Lotus should not surprise users with hidden fees after execution.

## How fees affect routing

Fees change the route comparison.

A route that looks best before fees may not be best after fees. Lotus should compare routes net of applicable fees so the selected path reflects what the user actually receives.

## What users should see

The preview and receipt should make fee impact clear.

Useful fields include:

- Gross expected output.
- Fee amount.
- Net expected output.
- Savings after fees.
- Final realized fee after settlement.

For simple retail views, Lotus can summarize this. Advanced views should expose the full calculation.

## Common mistakes

- Do not calculate savings before fees and display it as final.
- Do not apply a builder fee to a failed order unless the policy explicitly allows it.
- Do not hide builder fee impact from the receipt.
- Do not let fees turn a route into a worse execution than the baseline.

<Note>
  Builder fees are sustainable only when the user still receives a clear execution benefit.
</Note>
