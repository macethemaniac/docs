---
title: "Understanding surplus capture"
description: "How Lotus accounts for improvement beyond the baseline route."
---

Surplus is the improvement produced by a Lotus route compared with a reference baseline.

For a trader, surplus is the extra value created by better routing. For builders and liquidity partners, surplus is also the source of transparent fee and revenue policies.

## What creates surplus

Surplus can come from:

- Better venue depth.
- Multi-venue splitting.
- RFQ liquidity.
- Lower slippage.
- Better price discovery.
- Faster execution against fresh quotes.

The surplus calculation should be net of relevant fees.

## Baseline route

Lotus needs a baseline to measure improvement.

The baseline is usually the best safe single-venue route available for the same market, outcome, side, and amount at preview time.

If no reliable baseline exists, Lotus should avoid presenting a fake savings number.

## Estimated versus final surplus

Estimated surplus appears at preview time.

Final surplus is calculated after:

- Venue submit.
- Fill confirmation.
- Settlement verification.
- Fee accounting.
- Any fallback route handling.

These numbers can differ because markets move.

## Common mistakes

- Do not calculate surplus against a stale route.
- Do not count unreachable liquidity.
- Do not ignore builder fees.
- Do not show estimated surplus as final settlement.
- Do not reward a route that worsens the user's net outcome.

<Note>
  Surplus capture should align the protocol, builders, and users around better execution.
</Note>
