---
title: "Withdrawal flow"
description: "How Lotus returns available funds safely."
---

Withdrawals move available funds out of Lotus and back to the user's wallet.

Lotus should only withdraw funds that are not reserved for pending execution, settlement, or venue recovery.

## What can be withdrawn

Funds can usually be withdrawn when they are:

- Credited.
- Not locked by an open order.
- Not needed for settlement verification.
- Not reserved for an in-flight withdrawal.
- Available on the selected chain or venue path.

If funds are still propagating through a venue activation or order settlement, Lotus should wait.

## What users see

The withdrawal flow should show:

- Available amount.
- Destination wallet.
- Chain or rail.
- Estimated status.
- Final confirmation.

Users should not see internal relay or venue messages unless they need to take action.

## Why withdrawals can be blocked

Withdrawals can be blocked when:

- Funds are reserved for a pending order.
- Settlement verification is incomplete.
- The destination is invalid.
- A venue balance refresh is still pending.
- The withdrawal rail is not configured.

The UI should explain the concrete reason when the user can fix it.

## Common mistakes

- Do not withdraw from funds reserved for a live order.
- Do not assume a venue balance is withdrawable before reconciliation.
- Do not retry failed withdrawals without checking the failure reason.
- Do not expose raw provider errors in production copy.

<Warning>
  Withdrawal safety depends on correct accounting. Lotus should fail closed if balances are ambiguous.
</Warning>
