---
title: "Funding status"
description: "How to read deposits, activations, and venue-ready balances."
---

Funding status explains where your money is in the funding lifecycle.

The key distinction is available balance versus venue-ready balance. Available balance is cash Lotus can see. Venue-ready balance is cash that can be used by a specific venue route.

## Common statuses

You may see:

- `pending`: Lotus has recorded the request but confirmation is not complete.
- `credited`: Funds are available in the Lotus account view.
- `activating`: Lotus is enabling a venue-specific readiness step.
- `ready`: The venue can use the balance for execution.
- `failed`: The funding or activation step did not complete.
- `withdrawn`: Funds were returned to the destination wallet.

The exact label can vary by surface, but the meaning should stay clear.

## Why activation can take time

Some venues need an approval, account setup, or relay confirmation before funds are usable.

Lotus should poll the venue and update the status when readiness is confirmed. The user should not need to manually refresh many times.

## What to check first

If a venue is not ready, check:

- Whether the deposit settled.
- Whether activation was submitted.
- Whether the venue needs an allowance or account setup.
- Whether the venue balance was refreshed after activation.
- Whether the route uses a different venue than expected.

## Common mistakes

- Do not confuse funding wallet balance with venue-ready balance.
- Do not assume activation failed because it is still pending.
- Do not retry activation repeatedly while Lotus is polling.
- Do not withdraw funds that are reserved for in-flight execution.

<Note>
  A ready venue balance means that venue can be considered for routing. It does not guarantee a specific market quote.
</Note>
