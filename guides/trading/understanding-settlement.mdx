---
title: "Understanding settlement"
description: "How Lotus verifies fills after execution."
---

Settlement is the process of confirming that the trade result matches what Lotus submitted and what the venue reports.

An order is not fully accounted for just because it was submitted.

![Lotus order flow](/images/learn/lotus-order-flow.svg)

## What Lotus verifies

After execution, Lotus checks:

- Venue order ID.
- Filled size.
- Fill price.
- Order status.
- Venue position update.
- Wallet or balance movement.
- Route leg completion.
- Whether the final result matches the receipt.

For multi-leg routes, Lotus verifies every leg.

## Why this matters

Venue fills can arrive after the first submit response.

Without settlement verification, a user may see a pending order even though the venue filled it, or a filled order without the right position accounting. Lotus reconciles the venue result into trade history, settlement state, portfolio positions, and balances.

## What users should see

The UI should move toward one clear result:

- **Submitting** while Lotus is sending the order.
- **Submitted** while Lotus waits for venue confirmation.
- **Filled** after the venue fill is verified.
- **Failed** when the route cannot complete.

If recovery is needed, the user should see the concrete next step.

## Common mistakes

- Do not treat submit response as final accounting.
- Do not mark a fill without verifying the venue result.
- Do not leave real venue fills stuck as pending in the app.
- Do not combine settlement data from incompatible venue markets.

<Note>
  Settlement verification protects the account view from ghost fills and missing positions.
</Note>
